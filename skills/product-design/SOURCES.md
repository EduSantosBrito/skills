# Sources and licenses

This skill consolidates and deduplicates material from the following sources.

## Emil Kowalski design skills

- Repository: <https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills>
- Revision adapted by the original Product Design package: `049c92e3edc057d630ac07c99dc09ede830fd0dd`
- Source files: `emil-design-eng`, `apple-design`, `animation-vocabulary`, `review-animations`, `find-animation-opportunities`, `improve-animations`, and `pick-ui-library`, including their supporting standards and plan references.
- Adaptation: rewritten into one shared foundations reference, one authoritative motion reference, one vocabulary reference, one explicit library-selection reference, and one coherent specialist lens. Repeated guidance was removed; persona and canned-response behavior were not retained; version-sensitive performance claims were reframed as hypotheses that require profiling.
- License: MIT. The complete notice is preserved in `LICENSE-emilkowalski`.

## Revenue-Centric Design

- Repository: <https://github.com/heliocosta-dev/revenue-centric-design>

In particular, attribution must remain and the material may not be used for gambling, betting, casino, loot-box, or other real-money games-of-chance work.

## Taste Skill

- Repository: <https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill>
- Revision: `e988add20dab0fa97d7a76781c48961c8184288e`
- Source file: `skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md` (v2 experimental) and its changelog.
- Adaptation: selected brief-inference, qualitative visual-direction, anti-default, asset-honesty, and redesign ideas were rewritten into `visual-direction-and-anti-defaults.md` and `redesign.md`. Numeric dial presets, framework defaults, aesthetic blacklists, duplicated guardrails, canonical framework snippets, the unpopulated block-library contract, and the 62-item preflight checklist were not retained.
- Evidence note: the upstream changelog reports production testing, but the pinned repository does not publish a reproducible visual evaluation suite for each rule. Its aesthetic rules are therefore treated as contextual heuristics with explicit checks and overrides.
- License: MIT. The complete notice is preserved in `LICENSE-taste`.

## Editorial references

- [Agents with Taste](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/agents-with-taste) by Emil Kowalski: used as an editorial model for turning subjective craft into scenario, decision, rationale, and verification. Article prose is not copied; overlapping concrete motion guidance is grounded in the MIT-licensed Emil Kowalski skills above.
- Matt Pocock skills: <https://github.com/mattpocock/skills>, revision `84fdeffd12f2ee307994d1eb6feb48173b6e0502`, especially `skills/productivity/writing-for-agents`. Used for stage-aware invocation, context pointers, completion criteria, progressive disclosure, leading concepts, positive instructions, and single sources of truth. No Matt Pocock source file is copied into this package.
- User-provided `frontier-coding-agent-system-prompt.md`: used by the original adaptation as an editorial model for coherence, decision ownership, evidence, endpoint verification, and concise operational rules. No license was supplied for redistribution, so this package does not include that file.
