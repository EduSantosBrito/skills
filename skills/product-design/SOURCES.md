## Revenue-Centric Design

- Repository: <https://github.com/heliocosta-dev/revenue-centric-design>
- Current revision: `1942760c2242f609bf63ce2c97204f5a1791bd68` (v1.3.0, merged 2026-09-04; source coverage 2025-11-03 to 2026-08-26). Earlier adaptations used `6fa20cb4f91fa97bce9197be3f78b168784eb772`; every media link now points at the current revision.
- Original author of the underlying ideas and coined term: Richard ([@richardrx](https://x.com/richardrx)). The 2026 landing-page checklist record credits [@namyakhann](https://x.com/namyakhann), whose list Richard translated.
- Adaptation: Every Revenue-Centric Design record was mapped into topic references under a shared Decision / Use when / Action / Evidence / Source field set, with optional Source language and Visual fields retained where a record preserves a canonical quotation or source imagery, plus a shared evidence boundary. Philosophy references lead records with Use when and separate fields with em dashes; tactical topic references lead with Decision, use period separators, and may add Entry pointer and Application rule sections. The earlier 10-principle draft was retired in place; its surviving framings (every change as an experiment, the Swiss-army-knife cost warning, and cancellation beginning before intent) remain carried by the existing hypothesis/KPI, feature-discipline, and first-session-churn records. Large local media assets were replaced by stable links to the exact upstream revision.
- 2026-10-15 refresh: all 33 records added upstream in v1.2.0–v1.3.0 were adapted — 5 into the new `checkout-and-forms.md`, 3 into the new `dashboards-and-data-viz.md`, 8 into conversion, 4 into pricing, 4 into churn, 6 into product strategy, 2 into metrics, and 1 into onboarding. The v1.1.1 visual corrections and the v1.2.1/v1.3.0 fidelity corrections were applied. Records may carry an optional **Ethical boundary** field and caveats inside **Evidence**. Vendor percentages, survey figures, worked currency examples, and one company's results are labeled as perishable or illustrative, with their source; named behavioral mechanisms remain hypotheses. Where a primary source contradicts a post, the record follows the primary source: the Highrise record is conditional and states what the 37signals write-up tested, and the pricing-typography and tier-label record states the conditions of Motyka et al. (2016) and Wang et al. (2024), checked against their abstracts. Not adapted: an unsupported aside about gamified user-generated content on Chinese sites, and the uncited count of about 290 gamification mechanics.

## Taste Skill

- Repository: <https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill>
- Current revision: release tag `v2.0.0`, `2b44c81bbe36ac8a8a6bdd1c9ff593f28b8728f4` (2026-10-13), path `skills/taste-skill/` (v2 stable). `main` at `f624f37a8c650b9cc550253104925efe4c8de246` carried identical skill content; the immutable release was chosen.
- Earlier revision: `e988add20dab0fa97d7a76781c48961c8184288e`, `skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md` (v2 experimental, single file) and its changelog. The same tag freezes that file at `skills/taste-skill-v2-experimental/SKILL.md`; it is distinct from the stable skill.
- Source files: from the stable skill, `SKILL.md` and `references/research.md`, `copy.md`, `imagery.md`, `critique.md`, `motion.md`, and `tells.md`; from the experimental file, the original adaptation inputs.
- Adaptation: selected brief-inference, qualitative visual-direction, anti-default, asset-honesty, and redesign ideas were rewritten into `visual-direction-and-anti-defaults.md` and `redesign.md`. Numeric dial presets, framework defaults, aesthetic blacklists, duplicated guardrails, canonical framework snippets, the unpopulated block-library contract, and the 62-item preflight checklist were not retained.
- 2026-10-15 refresh (v2 stable, technique transfer only): `visual-direction-and-anti-defaults.md` gained the subject inventory, idea sentence, competitor test, conditional "one live thing", the removal-is-not-enough framing with tells treated as dated and contextual, an open-collections imagery procedure, and a look-and-correct loop on rendered screenshots that reuses the direct review contract; `conversion-and-landing-pages.md` gained the **Section argument** section (claims with proof decide section order); `design-primitives-and-tokens.md` gained the headline character-per-line estimate as a heuristic and meaning-based heading markup; `motion-and-interaction.md` gained progressive-enhancement reveals and the `grid-template-rows` accordion alternative. Not adapted: the house-style bans (eyebrows, monospace, numbering, hairlines, gradients, shadows, palettes, and similar), which are the owner's taste; the two-line and 32-character headline caps and statement-size limits; the scripts and their Node 22 and Chrome toolchain, the inspector and its rule set, the lint gate, the JSON library and the direction draw; mandatory picture floors (four or more real pictures), mandatory motion counts (eight or more moving elements), and fixed stagger and travel values; the fourteen-pass critique, its Awwwards-weighted score, caps, and three-round limit; numeric layout and type dials; the base CSS and JavaScript assets; and the release history. The experimental file's later motion-skeleton fixes (per-instance `useId` scoping, ascending `zIndex`, never combining `position: sticky` with a JavaScript pin, resize-aware horizontal pan distances, and naming transitioned properties instead of `transition: all`) were reviewed; their skeletons were not copied, and the generic guidance is already covered by the motion, performance, and foundations references.
- Evidence note: the stable release publishes its method in `references/research.md`. On 2026-10-05 it collected 248 gallery sites (One Page Love and Awwwards Sites of the Day); 247 loaded, 246 were re-measured, 235 were usable for scoring, and 202 were measurable for typography. Scores came from 21 model reviewers judging still frames, not from human jurors or real Awwwards scores; each site was captured once on one day, motion was inferred from frames, and the sample reflects recent gallery fashion as much as quality. The skill's own test rounds were also judged by model jurors, and its scores did not consistently beat a no-skill baseline. Its figures are therefore cited as context, not imported as benchmarks or causal findings. Upstream publishes the method and tooling, but this adaptation did not reproduce the study or its test suite; no independent human or product-outcome validation is supplied. Its aesthetic rules remain contextual heuristics with explicit checks and overrides.
- License: MIT, byte-identical at the earlier pin and at the release. The complete notice is preserved in `LICENSE-taste`.

## Editorial references

- [Agents with Taste](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/agents-with-taste) by Emil Kowalski: used as an editorial model for turning subjective craft into scenario, decision, rationale, and verification. Article prose is not copied; overlapping concrete motion guidance is grounded in the MIT-licensed Emil Kowalski skills above.
- Matt Pocock skills: <https://github.com/mattpocock/skills>, revision `84fdeffd12f2ee307994d1eb6feb48173b6e0502`, especially `skills/productivity/writing-for-agents`. Used for stage-aware invocation, context pointers, completion criteria, progressive disclosure, leading concepts, positive instructions, and single sources of truth. No Matt Pocock source file is copied into this package.
- User-provided `frontier-coding-agent-system-prompt.md`: used by the original adaptation as an editorial model for coherence, decision ownership, evidence, endpoint verification, and concise operational rules. No license was supplied for redistribution, so this package does not include that file.
- Design-fundamentals and primitive-vocabulary anchors (idea level; no text copied): Don Norman (affordance, signifier, mapping), Robin Williams (contrast, repetition, alignment, proximity), Josef Müller-Brockmann (grid systems), Edward Tufte (data-ink ratio), Matthew Butterick's *Practical Typography*, Nathan Curtis's design-token taxonomy, Tim Brown's modular type scales, and the W3C Design Tokens Community Group format. These inform `references/design-primitives-and-tokens.md` and the terminology in `references/foundations.md`.
